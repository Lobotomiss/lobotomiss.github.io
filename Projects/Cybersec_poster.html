Current topics

  • AI in CS
    • read more currently is AI a hot topic. not only in CS but everything. And the usescases in AI are many. This article mentions all these use cases!
  • EU digital omnibus
    • read more The digital omnibus of the EU describes the regulatory framework of everything digital in the EU. last march there has been an update to this document to include new rules regarding dataprotection, cybersecurity and AI.
  • NIS2
    • read more NIS is short for Network and information systems. The EU had released NIS1 in 2022, a directive regarding cybersecurity rules within this sector. The EU has recentrly updated this directive. This blog goes over the differences between the old and new directive.
  • COPPA
    • read more COPPA (Child Online Privacy Protection Act) is a new US regulation about how online sites should handle the privacy and access of children online. The COPPA has existed since 2000 but has been ammended in 2025 now broadening the definition of what data counts as personal information.
  • DORA & Sovereignty
    • read more DORA, short for Digital Operational Resilience Act and introduced in Januari 2025, is a recent act introduced into the EU as a response to the growing dependency on technology and (third party) tech companies. The dependency on tech companies can pose serious security issues when not handled correctly, and this poses an especially big threat in the financial sector.

    Due to global unrest there has also been a large push in the EU to move over to sovereign systems. meaning these systems are native to the EU. This strenghtens our cybersecurity against possible attacks from countries from outside the EU.
